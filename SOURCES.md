# Source map

1. Wu, Yu and Guo, *On the Complete Monotonicity of Rényi Entropy*, IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 71(7), 4895–4914 (2025), DOI 10.1109/TIT.2025.3561384. Source locators use [arXiv:2312.01819v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.01819v4), 1 November 2025: Section II-C, Fact 1, Conjecture 1 equation (19), Example 2 equation (20), Proposition 3. Its Tsallis conjecture is not confused with the Rényi title. Quadratic positivity and some failures above two are inherited.
2. Gu and Sellke, *A Counterexample to the Gaussian Completely Monotone Conjecture*, [2605.11656v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11656v1): Theorems 1–2, Proposition 1 and Gaussian-mixture justification. Prior Shannon failure, including log-concave existence. Continuity already refutes the joint interval assertion; the present paper is not the first such refutation.
3. Zou, Fan, Gao and Wang, *An Explicit Family of Log-Concave Counterexamples to the Gaussian Completely Monotone Conjecture*, [2608.30275v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30275v1): Theorem 1, periodic spectral grouping, Lemmas 3–4, equations (32)–(34), Corollary 5. Two frequencies, dominant spectral level, Gaussian localization and Cauchy filtering are inherited. The Tsallis tuning and multiplicative power-moment calculation are additional.
4. Wu and Yu, *Entropic Isoperimetric and Cramér–Rao Inequalities for Rényi–Fisher Information*, [2504.01837v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01837v2): Appendix B opening all-dimensional quadratic identity. This passage, not the entire long paper, was inspected.
5. Sun, *Concavity of Tsallis Entropy and Tsallis Entropy Power along Heat Flow*, [2601.12944v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12944v3): abstract/version and main concavity scope. Second-derivative results do not establish the full hierarchy. The complete sum-of-squares proof was not independently audited.

These exact versions were compared on 6 November 2026. refs.bib records bibliographic metadata. Cited papers are not distributed or relicensed in this package.
