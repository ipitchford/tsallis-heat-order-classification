# Scope and limitations

- The classification concerns universal alternating heat-entropy derivatives at every order, not typical distributions or empirical noise levels.
- Each fixed entropy order has its own threshold, and each asymptotic witness depends on the derivative index. There is no claim of one density failing every sufficiently high derivative.
- No uniform threshold is proved near entropy orders zero, one or two, or as the order tends to infinity.
- The displayed asymptotic family is strictly log-concave member by member. Its stated curvature lower bound tends to zero; no uniform curvature or variance bound is claimed for that family.
- The finite order-13 witness is smooth and strictly positive with Gaussian tails, but is not claimed log-concave or optimal in derivative order.
- Dilation preserves signs and allows variance normalization or a prescribed positive failure time separately. Their ratio is invariant, so both cannot be prescribed independently by the same dilation.
- Exact arithmetic checks the finite coefficients and numerical inequalities. Gaussian convolution, complex analytic continuation, Fourier filtering and Cauchy estimates remain written proof dependencies, not proof-assistant verification.
- The Shannon counterexamples, quadratic case and principal localisation mechanism are inherited. The claimed contribution is the pointwise positive-order classification, its order-dependent amplitude tuning and power-moment transfer, with a separately specified finite witness.
- The source comparison is bounded and cannot guarantee historical priority or exclude every differently named equivalent theorem.
- This is an AI-assisted unrefereed candidate. Internal checking is not external human review or unaffiliated reproduction.
